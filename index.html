Out of Station Visit — Counting Rules
An Out of Station Visit is counted when a trainer travels to a city different from their base city (city on record).
The round-trip distance must be more than 100 km to qualify as an Out of Station visit.
Each Out of Station visit with status "Done" is credited as 2 visits toward the trainer's daily done count (to account for travel effort).
The Target in this view is set at 3.0 visits per working day (adjusted for Out of Station credit).
The Out of Station Done column shows raw count of Out of Station visits completed. The Effective Done column reflects the count after the 2× Out of Station credit is applied.
